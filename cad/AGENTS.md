# CAD working rules

Read the active version's README before editing it. Current native SolidWorks work is in `v10 human editable/` (also read its `ASSEMBLIES.md` and `cad/solidworks.md`, the native modeling contract, path from repo root). The newer compact wheel + motor and its suspension pod are in `v11/` (build123d + STEP, assembled in SolidWorks from imported parts by `tools/sw_build.py`; not native features). `DUMMY_MOTOR.md` describes the fit/motion fixture. Current geometry, choices and known CAD/sim gaps live in the relevant design.md sections; consult those for design changes, not every tooling fix.

- Preserve `v7/`: earlier simulations read its BOM and head meshes. `v9/` is the frozen source wheel/prototype; do not silently overwrite it while editing v10.
- Native SolidWorks parts need named, commented global controls, readable design features, fully constrained sketches, real arcs and native patterns/mirrors. Do not replay CSG primitives as a nominally editable tree. Preserve Fernando's example part.
- Keep nominal geometry, completed checks and physical validation distinct. A successful part export does not certify a whole assembly, strength, printability or motion. Check all affected suspension/arm poses, opposite-hand components and interfaces. Do not claim an overall pass while known gaps remain.
- For build123d-based versions, dimensions come through `dims.py`. After changes, run that version's `build.py` checks and `render.py`: zero clashes, short threads and screw-access problems, one valid solid per print; inspect wall warnings. Follow version-specific prerequisites such as `build.py --render-only` before renders. For native v10, use its documented native validation tools; do not assume the older build pipeline applies.
- Print-first: PLA/PETG/TPU, roughly 256 mm bed. No heat-set inserts. Prefer M3 thread-forming into 2.8 mm pilots with at least 6 mm engagement; 3.4 mm through holes. Pan/flat heads only, not socket caps; shallow hex-drive M3 pan heads 6.5 × 2.5 mm. Fasteners are magenta.
- Model every purchased part's mating connector (real plug STEP) and its leads with the tightest allowed bend before packaging around it; keep about 4 mm clearance to moving parts and check it (`cad/solidworks.md` section 16).
- Update the model/export and relevant design baseline when adopted geometry changes. Keep validation outputs with the version; older assembly checks do not validate revised interfaces.
