The printed two-part dummy motor (stator + rotor, one 687 bearing, one M3 × 8 screw and one large M3 washer) uses M2.5 × 10 at both motor interfaces. A real AK40 uses the shorter vendor-interface screws (×6 output, ×8 rear). The wheel hub joints use M3 × 18. No heat-set inserts. See [DUMMY_MOTOR.md](DUMMY_MOTOR.md) for the motor assembly order.

The shock mockups slide freely and have no spring or damping. Their assembly mates limit eye-to-eye length to 40–50 mm. Fit real shocks for sprung testing.
