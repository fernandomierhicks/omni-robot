# SO-101 ↔ D435 calibration, sim study (Nov 5, 2026)

`studies/arm_calib.py` (solver: `ros/src/omni_calib/omni_calib/arm_calib.py`), raw rows in `arm_calib.json`.
Robot: current `ARM_D435` (`robot_arm_d435.json`), nose D435 colour camera 1280 × 720, 42° tall, 7.5° down. 5 seeds,
157 s on the PC.

## Set-up

- **Truth per seed:** the robot's chain perturbed.
  - Joint zeros: 3° each.
  - Each link: 1 mm and 0.5°.
  - Camera: 5 mm and 1.5°.
  - Sag past the encoders on lift / elbow / wrist flex: 0.4 / 0.4 / 0.6° per N·m **(est)**, at MuJoCo's gravity
    torques with a 25 g cube in hand.
- **Block:** a printed 40 mm cube, tag36h11 30 mm on three faces, in two variants:
  - **gripped:** its pose in the gripper unknown, 3 mm / 3°;
  - **keyed:** it locates on the gripper's own faces, 0.3 mm / 0.3°, and the roll zero is solved.
- **Poses:** random still poses, encoder ticks 4096 per turn. A pose is kept when the arm clears the robot and a tag is
  in the image, facing the camera within 65° and not hidden. Hidden tags were checked with a MuJoCo segmentation
  render of the D435 colour camera.
- **Corners:** the truth's projection plus noise: iid σ, and a shift shared by a tag's four corners at 0.6 σ. The
  Nov 4 bench measured 0.02–0.06 px on a still tag. Intrinsics are exact.
- **No detector:** the sim venv has none, so blur, focus and lighting are not modelled.
- **Score, at 30 fingers-down floor grasps 0.25–0.35 m from centre (tool 1 cm up):**
  - the camera sees the target, depth exact;
  - the model places it in base_link and the arm goes there by `omni_arm` IK;
  - the error is where the tool really ends up, horizontal and vertical. The IK's own 2 mm tolerance is taken out.

**Visibility:** only 1.9–2.4% of clear random poses showed a tag, never more than one at a time. The kept poses had the
tool 0.28–0.60 m out and 3–21 cm up, in front of the nose. The arm hid 36–39 otherwise visible tags over 1,200 poses.
A real session should plan its poses rather than sample them.

## Findings

1. **What it buys: the drawing misses by 7–33 mm (3° zeros), calibration by 0.3 mm (keyed block) to 1–2 mm
   (gripped).** The camera never sees the floor grasps; the model carries the fit there.
2. **A keyed block is worth it.** With a gripped block nothing ties the fingers to the block, so the fingers' place
   past the wrist stays at the drawing, mostly vertically (fit σ ~2 mm). Locating the block on the gripper's own faces
   pins it (σ 0.3 mm) and lets the roll zero be solved.
3. **Model the sag.** Without the sag terms the links take up part of the sag and the fit looks nearly as good
   (0.48 against 0.34 px), but the floor error grows 3–4× (keyed p95 0.28 → 1.18 mm). The sag size is an estimate:
   measure the real arm's (move, then push the held link and watch the tag).
4. **30–60 poses are enough at 0.3 px.** 120 adds little, and 1 px noise roughly doubles the error.
5. **Floor reach sits at the lift's range limit.** The fingers-down floor grasps need the shoulder lift near its
   1.745 rad limit. In seed 2 the calibrated (effective) lift zero was about −4°, and none of the 30 targets could be
   reached within the IK's 2° of vertical. 34–36 of 150 targets were unreachable across all seeds. The drawing's
   model believes it reaches them and misses instead. On the real arm:
   - after calibrating, re-check the floor reach;
   - record the lift's true range when calibrating (LeRobot records it by hand);
   - consider a small tilt tolerance for floor grasps, which `omni_arm`'s IK doesn't offer today.
6. **Gauges:** the pan and roll zeros trade exactly against the camera and the block. The lift, elbow and wrist zeros
   trade against link shifts (`arm_calib` docstring). The zero errors here are therefore not a score; the tool error
   is.

## Limits

- **The truth uses the solver's own model**, so these numbers are a best case for what the views can pin.
- **Not modelled:**
  - encoder nonlinearity;
  - backlash and deadband, and droop inside the servo loop (the settle-and-correct loop's job);
  - non-rigid links beyond the sag terms;
  - detection on real images, and the D435 colour intrinsics' error;
  - the gravity model's mass error;
  - the base's creep between the look and the grasp;
  - depth error.
- **Single runs per seed**, 5 seeds.
- **No hardware.** Next on the bench: print a keyed block, run 60 poses, then touch-test printed floor marks to measure
  the real floor error.
